Scanning a document

  1. Add your pages. Select Use camera and press the shutter once for each page; you can capture several in succession without stopping. Alternatively, import images you already have with the Choose images button, or drop them onto the dashed area.
  2. Set the corners. Drag the four handles to the corners of the page in the image. Local Scan straightens the area within them into a true rectangle, so a page photographed at an angle still comes out square.
  3. Choose a look. Document is generally best for printed text. Greyscale preserves shading, and Original leaves the colours unchanged.
  4. Arrange your document. Move your pages into your preferred order. You can rearrange them at any time before saving.
  5. Make it ready to use. Choose the format that suits your needs, whether a finished document, editable text, or the original pages. You can also copy the text and use it wherever you like.
  6. Save your document. Choose the format you need, then save, share, or send it to another app. You can also copy the text when you only need the words.

A browser only grants camera access to a page served over https:// or localhost. Which image formats can I bring in?

JPEG and PNG are supported everywhere. Most other common formats, including WebP, GIF and AVIF, open wherever your browser can decode them. HEIC and HEIF, the format iPhones use by default, open only in browsers that support them, which today mainly means Safari on Apple devices. If a file cannot be opened, Local Scan tells you rather than failing silently, and converting it to JPEG first always works.

Document mode made a photograph look wrong.

Document mode is designed for ink on paper. It measures how bright the paper is across the image and raises it to white, so a large, even area such as a clear sky or a block of shading may be interpreted as paper and turned white as well. For anything that is not a printed page, Greyscale or Original will give a truer result.

Which quality should I choose?

Balanced suits most scans. Smaller file is appropriate when you only need to read the text. Lossless (PNG) works best with the Document look, since the crisp black-and-white edges it produces are what JPEG compression handles least well.

What does "fit to image" mean?

Page size is a PDF setting. With fit to image, each page adopts the shape of its own picture, so nothing is cropped or padded. Local Scan determines the physical size by treating the picture as a 150 dpi scan, so a page photographed at close range appears close to its actual size. Choose A4 or US Letter instead when the file needs to print on standard paper; each page is then fitted and centred accordingly.

What are Word, Text and Markdown reading from?

Those formats, along with the Picture + searchable text PDF, require the words from the page rather than an image of them. Local Scan reads them using a text recogniser that runs on your device. The first time you use one, it downloads the recogniser once, roughly 6 MB, from the same source as the page, and works offline thereafter. Your pages are never involved. It reads English in this version and performs best on sharp, upright pages; if a page returns empty, applying the Document look under Adjust page usually helps.

What is a searchable PDF?

It is the same page image, with the recognised words placed beneath it. They are invisible, but you can select, copy and search them in your reader. They generally align with the printed words, close enough to highlight; where a page returns no positions, the words remain searchable, though not beneath their own characters. Recognition is never perfect, so it is best treated as a reliable way to search rather than an exact transcript.

Refunds for purchases made through the App Store, including Local Scan Pro, are handled by Apple, so we are unable to process one directly. You can request one from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Once the page has loaded, it continues to work with no connection, including the camera, processing and saving. The only exception is the text recogniser behind Word, Text, Markdown and searchable PDF, which loads once while you are online and then works offline as well.

Yes, from the outset. Everything the app requires comes with it, the text recogniser included, so capture, processing, reading and saving all work in Airplane Mode. Can I do it all from the keyboard?

Yes. Tab moves through every control. On a corner handle, the arrow keys adjust its position, and holding Shift moves it further. The up and down buttons on each page row reorder pages without dragging.
